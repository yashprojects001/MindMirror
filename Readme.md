# 🪞 MindMirror  
### *A mood-responsive web experience that reflects your inner state*

> “Sometimes you don’t need answers.  
> You just need a mirror.”

---

## 🌈 What is MindMirror?

**MindMirror** is an interactive web experience where your **mood controls the environment**.  
Select how you feel, and the app responds with:

- 🎨 Dynamic backgrounds  
- 🎧 Emotion-based ambient sounds  
- 📝 Thought-provoking reflections  
- ✨ Persistent highlights that stay even after reload  

This is not just a website.  
It’s a **quiet conversation with yourself**.

---

## 🎭 Supported Moods

| Mood | Visual | Sound | Experience |
|-----|------|------|-----------|
| 😌 Calm | Soft gradients | Rain / Piano | Slow & grounding |
| 🌧️ Sad | Dark tones | Ambient | Gentle & validating |
| 🌱 Curious | Fresh greens | Light synth | Explorative |
| 🌪️ Anxious | Muted purples | Breathing sound | Stabilizing |
| ☀️ Happy | Warm yellows | Lo-fi | Uplifting |

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 Mood-Driven UI
- The interface adapts instantly to your emotional state
- Backgrounds, text, and sounds change together

### 🎧 Ambient Sound Engine
- Looping, calming audio
- Enhances immersion without distraction

### ✍️ Reflection Space
- Editable text area
- Write freely or respond to guided prompts

### 💡 Persistent Highlight System
- Highlight any word or sentence
- Highlights **remain even after refresh or reopening the tab**
- Powered by `localStorage`

### 🧘 Minimal & Distraction-Free Design
- No clutter
- No notifications
- Just you and the mirror
